## Current Research Phase

The project is currently transitioning from **materials development** to **formal pretesting and experimental validation**.

The main experimental design is already specified, while the current stages focus on ensuring that the reasoning materials and confidence-language manipulation behave as intended before the main behavioral pilot.

## Public Repository Policy

The public repository documents the research design, implementation, analysis framework, and development process.

During active materials screening and data collection, the following are intentionally withheld:

- complete item banks;
- answer keys;
- final AI-correctness assignments;
- full incorrect-advice stimulus sets;
- internal reviewer feedback;
- participant-level data.

These materials may be released after the study is completed where appropriate.
